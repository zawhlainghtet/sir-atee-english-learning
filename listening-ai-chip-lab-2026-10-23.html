Inside an AI Chip Lab

Visit Amazon’s Annapurna Labs with BBC News and learn how custom processors are designed, tested, and used to power modern AI systems.

BBC News5 minutes 34 secondsTwo listens · about 15 minutes
▶ Play the BBC video

First Listen — Gist

Time: 6 minutes. Do not pause, use subtitles, or write full sentences. Listen for the overall message.

  1. What is the main purpose of the video?
  2. Why is Amazon designing its own chips?
  3. Is the report mainly optimistic, critical, or neutral about custom AI chips?

Second Listen — Details

Time: 8 minutes. Replay the video. You may pause briefly, but keep subtitles off until you finish the questions.

  1. Where is Annapurna Labs located?
  2. What tests must a new chip pass before it enters production?
  3. What does the phrase silicon bring-up describe?
  4. What price-performance improvement does Amazon claim for Trainium3?
  5. Name two of Amazon’s four chip families.
  6. How are Claude and ChatGPT used during chip development?
  7. What comparison explains the chip’s computing power?
  8. Which company still dominates global AI training chips?
  9. What major industry shift does the reporter identify at the end?

Vocabulary to Catch

  • workloada computing task processed by a system
  • validationtesting that confirms something works correctly
  • custom silicona processor designed for a specific organisation or purpose
  • vertical integrationcontrolling several connected stages of production
  • bottlenecka point that limits overall performance
  • data centrea facility containing servers and computing infrastructure

Writing Follow-Up — Priority Task

Time: 18 minutes · Target: 170–200 words

Question: Large technology companies increasingly design their own AI chips instead of relying entirely on external suppliers. Do the advantages of this development outweigh the disadvantages?

Required Structure

  1. Introduction: paraphrase the trend and give your position.
  2. Body 1: explain one major advantage with a concrete example.
  3. Body 2: explain one disadvantage, then evaluate whether it is stronger or weaker.
  4. Conclusion: restate your judgement without adding a new idea.

Check the main ideas only after completing both listens

The report shows Amazon’s Austin chip-design laboratory and explains how custom chips move from manufacturing through functional and performance testing into data centres. Amazon argues that controlling the chip, server, rack, and software layers improves cost and efficiency. The report also places this strategy in a wider competition involving Google, Microsoft, and the market leader Nvidia.
